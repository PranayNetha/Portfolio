Editing Approach

Seven decisions, made on every timeline.

Hook
Remove unnecessary pauses and rebuild the opening line so the first three seconds earn the next thirty.
Pacing
Keep the video moving without making it feel artificially fast — the cut serves the sentence, not a rhythm for its own sake.
Visuals
B-roll, screenshots, graphics and zooms — used when they add information or emphasis, skipped when they'd just be noise.
Captions
Clean, readable, and animated with intent — captions that support the words being said, not distract from them.
Sound
Dialogue cleanup, music beds, sound effects and level balancing so the video sounds as finished as it looks.
Color
Consistent, professional color correction across every clip in the timeline — no jumps in tone between cuts.
Story
Every cut is a decision. If it doesn't serve the message, it doesn't make the final timeline.

FAQ

Common questions.

What type of videos do you edit?+

Talking-head content specifically — YouTube videos, Reels, Shorts, podcasts and educational or business content built around someone speaking to camera.

Do you edit both long-form and short-form content?+

Yes. Long-form YouTube and podcast episodes, and short-form Reels/Shorts cut down from that same footage or delivered as standalone edits.

How do I send my footage?+

Through a shared drive link (Google Drive, Dropbox or similar) — details are confirmed once a project starts.

How many revisions are included?+

Two rounds of revisions are included with every edit, covering pacing, captions and any specific notes.

Can you match my existing editing style?+

Yes — send a few reference videos from your channel and the edit will match your existing pacing, caption style and tone.

Do you provide captions and motion graphics?+

Yes, animated captions and motion graphics are part of the standard edit, not an add-on.

Can you edit podcast videos?+

Yes — full episode editing plus short clips pulled for social from the same session.

Can you create Shorts/Reels from long-form videos?+

Yes, clipping and reformatting long-form footage into vertical short-form content is part of the service.
